For South African traders

Quotations to invoices, stock, and the books. One program.

TradeOS runs a trading business from the counter to the ledger: quotations, orders, invoices and credit notes, goods received, stock, who owes you and whom you owe, VAT, and the monthly books. Every PC keeps working when the connection or the power goes, and catches up when it is back.

YoursEach business's books are kept on that business's own server and its own PCs.
Backed upA locked copy of the books every day, checked against the server and kept in the business's own Google Drive.
Up to dateNew versions arrive by themselves and go in when TradeOS is restarted.

Getting TradeOS

TradeOS is installed and licensed by HFZY Developments for each business. The installer for the current version is on the releases page; it opens with a licence key from your supplier.

TradeOS and your Google account

If you choose to keep backups in Google Drive, TradeOS can reach only the backup files it puts there itself. The privacy policy says exactly what it does.
