What TradeOS does with your information

TradeOS keeps a trading business's books: on its PCs, in the TradeOS app on its phones, and on the business's own TradeOS server. This page says what it keeps, where, who sees it, and how to have it deleted. It applies to TradeOS for Windows and to the TradeOS app for Android, from 27 November 2026.

Who this is from

TradeOS is made by HFZY Developments, South Africa. Questions about this page, or a request about your information: sales.embassy1@gmail.com.

In short

  • The books belong to the business. They are kept on that business's own TradeOS server, in a Cloudflare account that belongs to the business, and on the PCs and phones that sign in to it.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no selling. TradeOS does not report what anybody does in it, does not show advertisements, and does not sell, rent or trade information to anybody. Nothing is used to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.
  • Encrypted on the way. Everything a PC or a phone sends to the business's server goes over an encrypted (TLS) connection.

What TradeOS keeps

Only what the people using it put into the books, and what is needed to sign them in:

  • The business's records: its customers and suppliers (names, contact people, telephone numbers, email and postal addresses, VAT numbers), its products (codes, descriptions, prices, stock, barcodes and pictures), its quotations, orders, invoices, credit notes, delivery notes and payments, and its accounts.
  • The people who use it: each one's name, the name they sign in with, and what they may do. A password is never kept as it was typed: it is turned into a check that cannot be turned back into the password, and the TradeOS app works that out on the phone, so the password itself is never sent.
  • Each PC and phone that signs in: a name for it (for a phone, its model, such as "Samsung SM-A546E") and a number TradeOS gives it, so that the business's licence can count its places and an owner can free a lost one.
  • On a delivery note signed on a phone: the customer's signature, kept as the lines of ink alone, with their name and the date.

Where it is kept

  • On the business's TradeOS server. Each business has its own, running on Cloudflare's network in a Cloudflare account that belongs to the business. HFZY Developments sets it up and keeps it up to date with the business's permission on that account, and looks at the books only when the business asks it for help.
  • On each PC and phone that signs in, a whole copy of the books, so that they open at once and keep working with no connection. On a phone it is kept in the TradeOS app's own private storage, which other apps cannot read, and it is left out of the phone's own backups.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. TradeOS does not give the books, or anything in them, to HFZY Developments or to any other party. What leaves a device goes only to the places the business itself has chosen:

  • its own TradeOS server, which Cloudflare runs for the business;
  • a customer or supplier the business sends a document to, by its own email, by WhatsApp or by whatever app the person sending chooses;
  • its own Google Drive, if the business keeps its backups there (below).

The TradeOS app on an Android phone

  • Joining: a phone joins the business's books by scanning a code shown on one of its PCs, and then the person signs in with the same name and password as on a PC. The phone takes one of the places the business's licence has for phones.
  • The passcode a person chooses on a phone is kept only on that phone, locked with a key in Android's own key store. It is never in the books, on the server, in a backup or sent anywhere.
  • The camera: TradeOS does not ask for the phone's camera itself. Scanning a barcode uses Google's code scanner, which is part of the phone (Google Play services), and gives TradeOS only the code it read. A product's picture is taken with the phone's own camera app or chosen from its gallery, and only the picture chosen is kept, made smaller first, as that product's picture in the books.
  • Printing and sending use Android's own printing and its own list of apps to send with: TradeOS hands over only the page that was chosen.
  • No signal: work done with no connection is kept on the phone, twice (in its copy of the books and in the app's own files, in case Android clears one), until the server has taken it.
  • A lost phone: an owner frees its place under Setup › Licence on a PC. From then on the server refuses that phone: it is signed out at its next contact and is given nothing more, and its passcode still stands in front of what it already has.
  • The demonstration company, which the app offers to anybody who has not joined a business yet, is made up: it carries HFZY Developments' own name, with invented customers, products and sales, on HFZY Developments' own server. Nothing in it is anybody's real business.

Backups in Google Drive

A PC with TradeOS can keep the backups of your books in your own Google Drive. This part says exactly what it can reach there, what it does with it, and where it is kept.

When TradeOS asks Google for anything

Only when someone chooses Connect Google Drive under Setup › Backups. Until then TradeOS has nothing to do with your Google Drive. Signing in happens on Google's own page in your browser: TradeOS never sees your Google password. Sending email is a separate thing, described under "Email" below.

What it can reach

  • Only the files it puts in your Drive itself. TradeOS asks Google for one permission, the narrowest there is for Drive: to see, create and delete the specific files it creates. Those are the backups of your books, in a folder called "TradeOS Backups". It cannot see, open, change or delete any other file or folder in your Drive.
  • The email address or name of the account you connect, so that TradeOS can show you which Google account your backups go to.

What it does with it

  • Sends each backup to that folder as soon as it is made.
  • Lists the backups it has put there, and removes the ones that are no longer kept: the newest of each of the last 30 days and of each of the last 12 months stay.
  • Shows the connected account's email address on the Backups screen.

Nothing else. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

Where it is kept

  • The permission Google gives TradeOS is kept only on the PC where you connected, protected by Windows for your Windows user. It is not sent to HFZY Developments or to anyone else.
  • The backups are locked on your PC with a passphrase you choose before they are sent. They are in your Drive, under your account. HFZY Developments has no access to your Drive, and without your passphrase nobody can open a backup.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. TradeOS does not send what it receives from Google to HFZY Developments, and does not share, sell or transfer it to any other party.

TradeOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Taking the permission away, and deleting

  • In TradeOS: Setup › Backups › Disconnect. TradeOS asks Google to withdraw the permission and forgets it.
  • At Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose TradeOS, and remove its access.
  • The backups already in your Drive are yours and stay there until you delete them: delete the "TradeOS Backups" folder to remove them all.

Email

TradeOS can send your invoices, statements and reports from your company's own mailbox. This is separate from Google Drive and uses none of the permissions above.

  • Only when someone sets it up, on the PC or the phone that should send: under Setup › Email on a PC, or under Me › Email from the company in the TradeOS app on a phone, by typing the mailbox's address and a password for it. For a Gmail mailbox that is an app password you make at Google for TradeOS alone, not the password you sign in to Google with.
  • What it does. It signs in to that mailbox's outgoing mail server to send the emails you choose to send, with the document attached. It does not read, list, move or delete any mail.
  • Where the password is kept. On that PC only, protected by Windows for your Windows user; or on that phone only, locked with a key in Android's own key store, which cannot be copied off the phone, and left out of the phone's own backups. It is not put in your books, it is not in a backup, it is never shown again once it is typed, and it is not sent to the TradeOS server, to HFZY Developments or to anyone else.
  • Taking it away. Setup › Email › Stop sending from this PC, or on a phone Me › Email from the company › Stop, forgets the mailbox and its password there. Removing the TradeOS app from a phone removes them too. A Gmail app password can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

How long it is kept, and having it deleted

  • The books are kept for as long as the business keeps them: tax law asks a business to keep its records for years, so a document that has been made final is never removed, and a customer or product that was used is closed rather than deleted. A business that stops using TradeOS keeps its server and books in its own Cloudflare account, and can delete them there.
  • A person who uses TradeOS is removed by the business's owner under Setup › Users on a PC; a phone or PC is taken off under Setup › Licence. Removing the TradeOS app from a phone removes its copy of the books and everything else the app kept on that phone.
  • A customer or supplier of a business who wants their details corrected or removed asks that business, which decides what its records must keep. Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act the business is responsible for that information, and HFZY Developments works for it.
  • Anything else: write to sales.embassy1@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Each business's books are on a server of their own and never mixed with another's. Every connection is encrypted. Signing in lasts 30 days on a device, and a four-digit passcode is asked each time TradeOS is opened again. What a person may see is decided by their role: staff who may not see cost prices do not see them, on a PC or a phone.

Children

TradeOS is for businesses and the people who work in them. It is not meant for children, and it does not knowingly keep information about children.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that widens what TradeOS can reach is asked for again before it takes effect: on Google's own page for Google Drive, and in an update of the app for a phone.
